## Platform

web

## Users
Two audiences with equal weight:
- **Recruiters and hiring companies** evaluating Octavio for a full stack role (remote or in Mendoza, Argentina).
- **Freelance clients** — businesses deciding whether to hire him to build their platform.

Both arrive with limited time, scan for evidence of real shipped work, then decide whether to make contact.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Octavio Curadelli, Full Stack Developer. Success means a visitor understands what he has built, trusts that he can deliver, and reaches out (email, LinkedIn, contact form).

## Positioning
He takes a product from zero to production on his own: design of the architecture, backend, frontend, payments, database and deployment. Hettacs (built solo in ~1 month, 500 registered users, Mercado Pago, own VPS), Naí Experience and Dusty Roots (sole developer alongside Nista Studio designers) are the proof.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- React 19 + TypeScript + Vite single page; Bootstrap 5 + Bootstrap Icons; content lives in `src/data/*.ts`.
- Sections: hero, sobre mí, experiencia, proyectos personales, educación/certificaciones/idiomas, habilidades, contacto (mailto form).
- Copy is in Spanish (Argentina, voseo).
- Personal projects section still contains placeholder entries; certificate Drive links pending.

## Brand Commitments
- Light/dark theme toggle must be kept.

## Evidence on Hand
- CV data: experience at IVCISA, Hettacs, Distanterra, Naí Experience, Dusty Roots; UTN degree; AWS and UTN.BA certifications.
- Project images on Cloudinary and `/assets/experiencia/`.
- No testimonials, client logos or metrics beyond those in the CV; do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Shipped work first: every claim is backed by a real project.
2. Ownership of the full cycle is the story, not a list of technologies.
3. Fast to scan for someone with two minutes.
4. Easy to contact from anywhere on the page.
